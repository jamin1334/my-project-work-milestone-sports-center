WHO WE ARE

Sport for growth, health and connection

Milestone Sports Centre is a community-focused sports and fitness centre in Kampala, Uganda. We aim to give young people and adults a welcoming place to be active, practise skills, enjoy friendly competition and make healthy movement part of everyday life.

We believe participation matters as much as performance. Every session can be a chance to learn something new, support a teammate and celebrate progress.

Our mission

To encourage active lifestyles by offering accessible, positive sports and fitness activities in a safe and respectful environment.

Our vision

To grow into a trusted community sports centre where people of different backgrounds can develop their potential.

Our values

Respect, teamwork, consistency, inclusion and fair play guide the way we train and participate.

SERVICES & PROGRAMMES

Activities to support your goals

Below is an overview of the services visitors can ask about. Programme availability should be confirmed with the centre.

⚽

Football training

Skill sessions can cover passing, dribbling, first touch, shooting, positioning and small-sided games. Group activities also encourage communication, discipline and teamwork.

Suitable for: beginners and developing players

🏀

Basketball practice

Build fundamentals through ball handling, passing, shooting form, footwork, defensive movement and friendly game situations. Sessions can be adapted to experience level.

Suitable for: youth and adult participants

💪

Fitness & conditioning

General fitness activities may include warm-ups, body-weight exercises, mobility, cardiovascular conditioning and cool-downs. Participants should work within their abilities.

Focus: stamina, mobility and strength

🧭

Coaching & skill development

Goal-oriented practice helps participants build technique, confidence, sportsmanship and consistent training habits. Ask about suitable group options and coaching availability.

Focus: learning and steady progress

GOOD TO KNOW

Before your first session

What to bring

Comfortable sportswear, suitable footwear, drinking water and any personal equipment requested for your activity.

Warm up and stay hydrated

Arrive a little early when possible, warm up before activity, take breaks and tell the session lead if you feel unwell or injured.

Ask before you visit

Session times, prices, equipment access and spaces may vary. Contact the centre to confirm current details.
