Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about visiting and joining Milestone Sports Centre.

What are the operating hours of Milestone Sports Centre?

We are open Monday to Saturday from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Sunday sessions are reserved for special community tournaments and private bookings.

Do I need to register in advance before attending a training session?

While walk-ins are welcome for general fitness, we recommend contacting us or checking our schedule ahead of time to confirm slot availability for structured football and basketball practices.

What items should I bring for my first sports training visit?

Please bring comfortable sportswear, appropriate athletic footwear (sneakers or turf boots), a refillable water bottle, and a towel.

Are coaching programmes suitable for complete beginners?

Yes! Our training sessions cater to all proficiency levels, from absolute beginners looking to learn fundamentals to developing players seeking to sharpen their skills.

How can I contact the centre for group bookings or private inquiries?

You can reach us through our Contact Page, call us directly at +256 749 878 629, or send an email to info@milestonesports.com.
